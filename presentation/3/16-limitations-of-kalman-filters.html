Limitations of Kalman filters

p(xk | zk) ≈ N(x̂k|k, Pk|k)

Every Kalman-type filter carries the belief in two numbers. That choice has consequences, whatever the model.

A Gaussian is imposed

The posterior is replaced by its first two moments at every step. A nonlinear map of a Gaussian is not Gaussian: skew and multiple modes are discarded.

Optimal only among linear estimators

The correction is linear in the innovation, so the filter is LMMSE, not MMSE. Even exact moments do not yield the true conditional mean.

No hard constraints

A Gaussian puts probability on every value, including impossible ones: a negative distance, a pose inside a wall. Clamping the estimate fixes the number, not the covariance.

When one mean and one covariance are not enough, change the representation: mixtures of Gaussians, particle filters, or different coordinates.
