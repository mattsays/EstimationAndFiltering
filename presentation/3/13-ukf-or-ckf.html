UKF or CKF?

Same predict–correct structure. Different rules for propagating uncertainty.

UKF: flexibility

2n + 1 points in the standard scaled rule.

α, β, κ control point spread and weights.

Choose when you want to test sampling choices and can validate the tuning.

CKF: a fixed rule

2n points, each weight = 1/(2n).

Fixed symmetric pairs; positive equal weights.

Choose when you want fewer sampling decisions and a standard implementation.

Same tracking problem. Different uncertainty approximations.

Compare accuracy, reliability of uncertainty estimates and runtime on the same data.

Why can they look similar?

Both transform representative points through the nonlinear model.

With α = 1, β = 0, κ = 0, the standard UKF has the same effective points and weights as CKF. Its central point has zero weight.
