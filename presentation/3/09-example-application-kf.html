Example Application for KF

1

Predict with the motor model

Applied voltage and the previous state estimate predict the next angle, angular speed and armature current.

2

Observe with the encoder

The encoder reads the shaft angle. Measured minus predicted angle is the innovation.

3

Correct the estimated state

The KF weighs model and encoder by their uncertainty and updates angle, speed and current.

4

Why KF?

With constant parameters, linear friction and no saturation, the motor dynamics and the angle measurement are linear. No linearization needed.
