Example Application for EKF

1. Predict with wheel odometry
Wheel encoders estimate movement. The motion model predicts position and heading; wheel errors add uncertainty.
2. Observe with an external sensor
The sensor measures range and bearing to known landmarks.
Measured minus predicted observations give the innovation.
3. Correct the estimated pose
EKF combines the prediction with the landmark observations, weighted by uncertainty. The estimate shifts, not the physical robot.
Why EKF?
Motion uses sin/cos of heading; landmark range and bearing are nonlinear.
EKF linearizes locally around the estimated pose, a good fit when errors stay small.
