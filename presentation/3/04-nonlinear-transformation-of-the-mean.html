Nonlinear transformation of the mean

\(x\sim\mathcal{N}(0,1),\qquad y=f(x)=x^2+\tfrac12\)

What is the output mean \(\mathbb{E}[y]\)?

First-order Taylor approximation at zero.

\[f(x)\approx f(0)+f'(0)(x-0)=\tfrac12\] \[\widehat{\mu}_y^{\mathrm{EKF}}=\mathbb{E}[\tfrac12]=\boxed{\tfrac12}\]

Weighted average of the nonlinear outputs.

\[\chi_1=-1,\quad\chi_2=1,\quad w_1=w_2=\tfrac12\] \[\widehat{\mu}_y^{\mathrm{CKF}}=\sum_{i=1}^{2}w_i f(\chi_i)=\boxed{\tfrac32}\]
\(\text{Exact mean:}\quad\mathbb{E}[y]=\mathbb{E}[x^2]+\tfrac12=1+\tfrac12=\boxed{\tfrac32}\)

CKF recovers the exact mean in this example. This does not imply an exact output variance.
