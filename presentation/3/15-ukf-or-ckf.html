UKF or CKF?

Why keep UKF? Tunable sampling can improve accuracy on a specific problem.

UKF: flexibility

2n + 1 points in the standard scaled rule.

λ controls point spread and mean weights; ω₀ corrects the central covariance weight.

Tuning can reduce higher-order approximation errors and outperform the fixed CKF rule on some nonlinear problems.

CKF: a fixed rule

2n points, each weight = 1/(2n).

Fixed symmetric pairs; positive equal weights.

A simpler sampling choice with no spread tuning. Positive weights alone do not guarantee the best accuracy.
