Example Application for UKF

Conceptual illustration: point layouts and mean path are schematic. The rocket represents the estimate, not ground truth.

1

Track a turning target

Five states: position, velocity and turn rate. The unknown turn rate makes the motion model nonlinear.

2

Spread the sigma points

Generate 2n + 1 = 11 points from x̂ and P. Each represents a full state: position, velocity and turn rate. The point cloud here is an abstract illustration.

3

Propagate and recombine

Transform all 11 points through the motion model. Their weighted mean gives the predicted state; their weighted spread gives its covariance. The blue curve illustrates the evolution of the estimated mean.

4

Why UKF?

No model Jacobians. λ sets point spread and mean weights; c₀ adjusts the central covariance weight. CKF handles the same task with a fixed rule.
