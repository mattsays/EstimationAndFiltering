Kalman Filter Recap

1. Prediction \(k \to k+1\)

Predicts a-priori moments (mean & covariance) for step \(k+1\).

\[ \hat{x}_{k+1|k} = F_k\hat{x}_{k|k} + G_ku_k \] \[ P_{k+1|k} = F_kP_{k|k}F_k' + Q_k \]
\(\hat{x}_{k+1|k}, P_{k+1|k}\) 2 A-Priori Moments
Measurement Model
\( z_{k+1} = H_{k+1} x_{k+1} + v_{k+1} \)

2. Correction of \(k+1\) Prediction

Updates a-posteriori moments via LMMSE.

\[ K_{k+1} = P_{k+1|k}H_{k+1}'S_{k+1}^{-1} \] \[ \hat{x}_{k+1|k+1} = \hat{x}_{k+1|k} + K_{k+1}(z_{k+1} - \hat{z}_{k+1|k}) \] \[ P_{k+1|k+1} = P_{k+1|k} - K_{k+1} S_{k+1} K_{k+1}' \]
Next Step \(k+1 \to k+2\) \(\hat{x}_{k+1|k+1}, P_{k+1|k+1}\)
