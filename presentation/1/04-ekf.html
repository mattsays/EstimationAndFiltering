Extended Kalman Filter (EKF)

Deriving the prediction step via Taylor expansion

1. Non-linear dynamics

The true state evolves through a non-linear \(f_k(\cdot)\).

\(\displaystyle x_{k+1} = f_k(x_k) + w_k \)
→

2. First-order Taylor expansion

Linearize around the last estimate \(\hat{x}_{k|k}\), with Jacobian \(F_k = \left.\frac{\partial f_k}{\partial x_k}\right|_{x_k = \hat{x}_{k|k}}\):

\(\displaystyle f_k(x_k) \approx f_k(\hat{x}_{k|k}) + F_k(x_k - \hat{x}_{k|k}) \)
↓

3. EKF prediction step

Taking the expectation \(\mathbb{E}[\cdot]\) of the linearized model gives the a priori moments:

State prediction
\(\displaystyle \hat{x}_{k+1|k} = f_k(\hat{x}_{k|k}) \)
Covariance prediction
\(\displaystyle P_{k+1|k} = F_kP_{k|k}F_k' + Q_k \)
