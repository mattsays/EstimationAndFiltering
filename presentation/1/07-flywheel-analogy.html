The Flywheel Analogy

Gaussian \(\to\) 2 moments \(\sim \) mass & inertia \(\to\) Flywheel

2D Gaussian distribution

\(\displaystyle \mathbf{\hat{x}} = \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}], \quad \mathbf{P} = \mathbb{E}[(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{\hat{x}})(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{\hat{x}})^T] \)
↔

Flywheel of mass \(M=1\text{kg}\)

\(\displaystyle \mathbf{c_m} = \sum_{i} m_i \mathbf{r}_i, \quad \mathbf{I} = \sum_{i} m_i \|\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{c_m}\|^2 \)
\(\int p(\mathbf{x})d\mathbf{x} = 1\) ↔ Mass \(M = 1\text{kg}\)
Mean \(\mathbf{\hat{x}}\) ↔ Center of Mass \(\mathbf{c_m}\)
Covariance \(\mathbf{P}\) ↔ Inertia \(\mathbf{I}\)
