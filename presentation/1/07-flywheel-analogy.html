The Flywheel Analogy

Gaussian \(\to\) 2 moments \(\sim\) mass & moment of inertia \(\to\) Flywheel

2D Gaussian distribution \(\int p(\mathbf{x}) \, d\mathbf{x} = 1\)

1st Moment (Mean)
\(\begin{aligned} \mathbf{\hat{x}} &\:=\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}] \\ &\:=\int \mathbf{x} \, p(\mathbf{x}) \, d\mathbf{x} \end{aligned}\)
2nd Moment (Covariance)
\(\begin{aligned} \mathbf{P} &\:=\mathbb{E}[(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{\hat{x}})(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{\hat{x}})^T] \\ &\:=\int (\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{\hat{x}})(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{\hat{x}})^T p(\mathbf{x}) \, d\mathbf{x} \end{aligned}\)
↔

Flywheel of mass \(M = \int \rho(\mathbf{r})\,d\mathbf{r} = 1\text{kg}\)

1st Moment (Center of Mass)
\(\begin{aligned} \mathbf{c_m} &\:=\int \mathbf{r} \, \rho(\mathbf{r}) \, d\mathbf{r} \end{aligned}\)
2nd Moment (Inertia Tensor)
\(\begin{aligned} \mathbf{I} &\:=\int (\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{c_m})(\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{c_m})^T \rho(\mathbf{r}) \, d\mathbf{r} \end{aligned}\)
Discrete Form
\(\displaystyle \mathbf{c_m} = \sum_i m_i \mathbf{r}_i \quad \mathbf{I} = \sum_i m_i (\mathbf{r}_i-\mathbf{c_m})(\mathbf{r}_i-\mathbf{c_m})^T\)
\(\int p(\mathbf{x})d\mathbf{x} = 1\) ↔ Mass \(M = 1\text{kg}\)
Mean \(\mathbf{\hat{x}}\) ↔ Center of Mass \(\mathbf{c_m}\)
Covariance \(\mathbf{P}\) ↔ Moment of Inertia \(\mathbf{I}\)
