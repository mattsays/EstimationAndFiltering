From Flywheel to Sigma Points

Smashing the continuous mass into 5 discrete, weighted points

Flywheel of mass \(M=1\text{kg}\)

\(\displaystyle \mathbf{c_m} = \sum_i m_i \mathbf{r}_i \quad \mathbf{I} = \sum_i m_i (\mathbf{r}_i-\mathbf{c_m})(\mathbf{r}_i-\mathbf{c_m})^T\)
→

5 Sigma Points

\(\displaystyle \mathbf{\hat{x}} = \sum_{i=0}^{4} w_i \mathbf{x}_i \quad \mathbf{P} = \sum_{i=0}^{4} w_i (\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{\hat{x}})(\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{\hat{x}})^T \)

The weights \(w_i\) perfectly replicate the distribution's moments:

1. Total Probability
\(\sum_i w_i = 1\)
2. Center of Mass (Mean)
\(\sum w_i \mathbf{x}_i = \mathbf{\hat{x}}\)
3. Inertia (Covariance)
\(\sum w_i (\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{\hat{x}})(\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{\hat{x}})^T = \mathbf{P}\)
