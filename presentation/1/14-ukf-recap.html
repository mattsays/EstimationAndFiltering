Unscented Kalman Filter Recap in Action

Full cycle: Sigma Points Extraction \(\to\) Non-Linear Propagation \(\to\) Gaussian Reconstruction

1. Extract Sigma Points

Deterministic sampling from the prior:

\(\displaystyle \mathbf{x}_0 = \mathbf{\hat{x}}_{k|k} \)
\(\displaystyle \mathbf{x}_i = \mathbf{\hat{x}}_{k|k} \pm \left ( \sqrt{(n+\lambda) \mathbf{P}} \right )_i \)
→

2. Non-Linear Function

Each point flows through \(f(\cdot)\):

\(\displaystyle \mathbf{y}_i = f(\mathbf{x}_i) \)
→

3. Reconstruct Gaussian

Compute predicted mean and covariance:

\(\displaystyle \mathbf{\hat{x}}_{k+1|k} = \sum_{i=0}^{2n} w_i \mathbf{y}_i \)
\(\displaystyle \mathbf{P}_{k+1|k} = \sum_{i=1}^{2n} w_i (\dots)(\dots)^T + w_0^c (\dots)(\dots)^T \)

Output: A-Priori Moments \(\left( \mathbf{\hat{x}}_{k+1|k}, \mathbf{P}_{k+1|k} \right)\)

These serve as the prediction step, ready for the LMMSE Correction Update with measurements \(z_{k+1}\)
