The radial rule
One Gauss–Laguerre point fixes the radius of the cubature points
The integral
Radial part of the Gaussian-weighted integral.
\(\displaystyle \int_0^\infty f(x)\,x^{n-1}e^{-x^2}dx\)
→
1. Change of variable
Put \(t=x^2\), \(\tilde f(t)=f(\sqrt t)\): the integral is ½ × this.
\(\displaystyle \int_0^\infty \tilde f(t)\,t^{n/2-1}e^{-t}dt\)
→
2. First-degree rule
Approximate it with one point \(t_1\) and one weight \(\omega_1\).
\(\displaystyle \approx\omega_1\,\tilde f(t_1)\)
↓
3. Match \(\tilde f=1\) and \(\tilde f=t\), the intersections with the unti sphere
Odd-degree terms vanish by symmetry once combined with the spherical rule.
\(\displaystyle \omega_1=\Gamma(\tfrac n2)\)
→
\(\displaystyle t_1=\tfrac{\Gamma(n/2+1)}{\Gamma(n/2)}=\tfrac n2\)
→
\(\displaystyle \tfrac12\Gamma(\tfrac n2)\,f(\sqrt{n/2})\)
