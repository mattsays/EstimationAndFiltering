What is a Cubature?
Integration as a weighted sum of function values
Quadrature (1D)
Weights \(w_i\) and nodes \(x_i\) on a line
\(\displaystyle \int_a^b f(x)\,dx \approx \sum_i w_i f(x_i)\)
→
Cubature (n-D)
Weights \(w_k\) and sigma points \(x_k\) in space
\(\displaystyle \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} f(x)\,w(x)\,dx \approx \sum_k w_k f(x_k)\)
