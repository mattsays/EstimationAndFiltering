CKF: Time Update
From the posterior at k|k to the predicted state at k+1|k
1 · Factorize
Posterior at time k is known
\(\displaystyle P_{k|k} = S_{k|k}S_{k|k}^{T}\)
→
2 · Cubature points
\(i = 1, 2, \ldots, m\), with \(m = 2n_x\)
\(\displaystyle \mathbf{x}^{i}_{k|k} = S_{k|k}\xi^{i} + \hat{x}_{k|k}\)
→
3 · Propagation
Through the state transition \(f\)
\(\displaystyle \mathbf{x}^{*i}_{k+1|k} = f(\mathbf{x}^{i}_{k|k}, u_k)\)
↓
4 · Predicted state
Mean of the propagated points
\(\displaystyle \hat{x}_{k+1|k} = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathbf{x}^{*i}_{k+1|k}\)
→
5 · Predicted error covariance
Includes the process noise covariance \(Q_k\)
\(\displaystyle P_{k+1|k} = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathbf{x}^{*i}_{k+1|k}\mathbf{x}^{*i\,T}_{k+1|k} - \hat{x}_{k+1|k}\hat{x}_{k+1|k}^{T} + Q_k\)
