CKF: Measurement Update
From the predicted state at k+1|k to the updated state at k+1|k+1
1–2 · Cubature points
\(\displaystyle P_{k+1|k} = S_{k+1|k}S_{k+1|k}^{T}\)
\(\displaystyle \mathbf{x}^{i}_{k+1|k} = S_{k+1|k}\xi^{i} + \hat{x}_{k+1|k}\)
→
3–4 · Measurement
\(\displaystyle \mathcal{Z}^{i}_{k+1|k} = h(\mathbf{x}^{i}_{k+1|k}, u_{k+1})\)
\(\displaystyle \hat{z}_{k+1|k} = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathcal{Z}^{i}_{k+1|k}\)
→
5–6 · Covariances
\(\displaystyle P_{zz,k+1|k} = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathcal{Z}^{i}_{k+1|k}\mathcal{Z}^{i\,T}_{k+1|k} - \hat{z}_{k+1|k}\hat{z}_{k+1|k}^{T} + R_{k+1}\)
\(\displaystyle P_{xz,k+1|k} = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathbf{x}^{i}_{k+1|k}\mathcal{Z}^{i\,T}_{k+1|k} - \hat{x}_{k+1|k}\hat{z}_{k+1|k}^{T}\)
↓
7 · Kalman gain
Weights the innovation in the update
\(\displaystyle K_{k+1} = P_{xz,k+1|k}\,P_{zz,k+1|k}^{-1}\)
→
8–9 · State update
\(\displaystyle \hat{x}_{k+1|k+1} = \hat{x}_{k+1|k} + K_{k+1}\,(z_{k+1} - \hat{z}_{k+1|k})\)
\(\displaystyle P_{k+1|k+1} = P_{k+1|k} - K_{k+1}\,P_{zz,k+1|k}\,K_{k+1}^{T}\)
