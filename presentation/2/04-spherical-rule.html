The spherical rule
2n symmetric points on the unit sphere fix the direction of the cubature points
The integral
Directional part of the Gaussian-weighted integral over the unit sphere.
\(\displaystyle \int_{U_n} f(y)\,d\sigma(y)\)
→
1. Symmetric rule
2n points \(\pm u\,e_j\) on the axes, all with the same weight \(\omega\).
\(\displaystyle \approx\ \omega\sum_{i=1}^{2n} f([u]_i)\)
→
2. Symmetry
The point set is invariant to permutations and sign changes.
\(\displaystyle \textstyle\sum_i d_i\ \text{odd}\ \Rightarrow\ 0\)
↓
3. Match the even monomials \(f=1\) and \(f=y_1^2\)
Odd monomials are already exact, so only degrees 0 and 2 remain; \(A_n=2\pi^{n/2}/\Gamma(n/2)\) is the sphere's area.
\(\displaystyle 2n\,\omega=A_n\)
→
\(\displaystyle 2\,\omega\,u^2=\tfrac{A_n}{n}\)
→
\(\displaystyle u^2=1,\ \ \omega=\tfrac{A_n}{2n}\)
