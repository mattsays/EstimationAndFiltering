CKF → SCKF: What Changes
No factorization step: square roots S are propagated instead of P, how to derive them?
Time update · covariance
CKF
\(\displaystyle P_{k+1|k} = \mathbf{X}^{*}_{k+1|k}\mathbf{X}^{*T}_{k+1|k} + Q_k\)
SCKF
\(\displaystyle S_{k+1|k} = \mathrm{Tria}\big([\,\mathbf{X}^{*}_{k+1|k}\;\; \sqrt{Q_{k}}\,]\big)\)
Measurement · innovation cov.
CKF
\(\displaystyle P_{zz,k+1|k} = \mathbf{Z}_{k+1|k}\mathbf{Z}_{k+1|k}^{T} + R_{k+1}\)
SCKF
\(\displaystyle S_{zz,k+1|k} = \mathrm{Tria}\big([\,\mathbf{Z}_{k+1|k}\;\; \sqrt{R_{k+1}}\,]\big)\)
Measurement · Kalman gain
CKF
\(\displaystyle K_{k+1} = P_{xz,k+1|k}\,P_{zz,k+1|k}^{-1}\)
SCKF
\(\displaystyle K_{k+1} = \big(P_{xz,k+1|k}\,/\,S_{zz,k+1|k}^{T}\big)\,/\,S_{zz,k+1|k}\)
Measurement · updated covariance
CKF
\(\displaystyle P_{k+1|k+1} = P_{k+1|k} - K_{k+1}P_{zz,k+1|k}K_{k+1}^{T}\)
SCKF
\(\displaystyle S_{k+1|k+1} = \mathrm{Tria}\big([\,\mathbf{X}_{k+1|k} - K_{k+1}\mathbf{Z}_{k+1|k}\;\; K_{k+1}S_{R,k+1}\,]\big)\)
\(\mathbf{X}^{*}_{k+1|k} = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{m}}\,[\,\mathbf{x}^{*,1}_{k+1|k}-\hat{x}_{k+1|k}\;\cdots\;\mathbf{x}^{*, m}_{k+1|k}-\hat{x}_{k+1|k}\,]\)
\(\mathbf{Z}_{k+1|k} = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{m}}\,[\,\mathcal{Z}^{1}_{k+1|k}-\hat{z}_{k+1|k}\;\cdots\;\mathcal{Z}^{m}_{k+1|k}-\hat{z}_{k+1|k}\,]\)
